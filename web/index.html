    How to play

    One question a day. Estimate the number in six guesses.

    After each guess you learn which way to go and roughly how far off you are:

    • 🟩 within 10%: you win
    • 🟨 within 2×
    • 🟧 within 10×
    • 🟥 more than 10× off

    Type numbers however you like: 4200, 4,200, 2.5 million, 3e9, 10^20.

    A new question appears at midnight. Every answer comes with its source.
